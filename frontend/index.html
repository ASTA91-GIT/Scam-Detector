Threat Horizon

Not Every Job Offer
Is What It Seems.

Sophisticated threat actors weaponize fake executive branding, lookalike domains, and psychological urgency traps. These are actual deceptive patterns identified in digital employment scams:

"PAY ₹5,000 REGISTRATION FEE BEFORE LAPTOP DISPATCH"
Advance-Fee Weaponization: Scammers masquerade as corporate HR demanding mandatory hardware deposits via non-reversible UPI or wire transfer.
"URGENT: ACCEPT WITHIN 30 MINUTES OR OFFER IS FORFEITED"
Psychological Coercion: Artificial deadlines induce panic to prevent the candidate from conducting independent verification with the real employer.
"SELECTED FOR $65/HR ROLE WITHOUT ANY FORMAL INTERVIEW"
Disproportionate Compensation: Inflated pay for trivial remote data entry designed to lure candidates into identity theft or money mule rings.
"RECRUITER ON TELEGRAM: SEND BANK PASSBOOK & GOVT ID"
Off-Grid Communication: Threat actors steer candidates away from verifiable corporate email onto Telegram, WhatsApp, or Signal.

Corporate Verification

Know Who Is
Behind The Offer.

Scammers frequently clone legitimate corporate names while conducting communications through spoofed lookalike domains or free public webmail.

  • MX & DNS Cross-Referencing: Validates whether recruiter mail servers match authentic corporate records.
  • Lookalike Typosquatting Detection: Spots malicious permutations like company-hr-careers.biz.
  • Physical Registry Lookups: Confirms company legal identity and registered headquarters.
